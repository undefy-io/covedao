covs.trade is a fair-launch token launchpad on Bitcoin mainnet. Every token has 21,000,000 supply, no team tokens, and its own **covenant vault**: a Taproot address with no spending key that holds the BTC buyers paid. Other Bitcoin launchpads keep that BTC in a single-key treasury wallet. We lock it in script.

Because the BTC stays in the vault, **there is always a buyer**. Any holder can sell back to the vault for real BTC at the curve price, whenever they want. Creators earn **50% of every mint**, paid on-chain in the same transaction, and after mint-out holders trade peer-to-peer with **presigned listings**: the seller signs once, and a buyer can fill the listing without the seller being online.

Everything is checkable. Every launch, mint, sell-back, transfer and trade is an ordinary Bitcoin transaction, and the indexer is open and deterministic: anyone who runs it on the same blocks gets the same balances and the same state hash.
